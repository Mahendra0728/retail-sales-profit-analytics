# Retail Sales & Profit Analytics

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to evaluate sales performance, profitability, regional performance, product-level trends, and business factors affecting profit.

The analysis was performed using Python and Power BI on a dataset containing over 50,000 records. Python was used for data cleaning, preprocessing, feature engineering, and exploratory data analysis (EDA), while Power BI was used to develop an interactive dashboard for visualizing business performance.

## Key Business Insights
- Higher discount levels were generally associated with lower profitability.
- Orders with discounts above 40% recorded an average negative profit of approximately 90.15 per order.
- The Central region generated the highest sales and profit.
- Technology recorded the highest total sales and profit among the product categories.
- Sales peaked during November and December, indicating stronger year-end demand.
- The Consumer segment contributed the highest total profit.
- High sales do not always guarantee high profitability, as discounts and associated costs can affect profit margins.
